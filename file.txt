I want to build a full-stack web application called AgriBid (working title), a transparent agricultural procurement marketplace that connects Farmers, Suppliers/Wholesalers, and Customers. The goal is to eliminate unfair pricing practices by making procurement transparent and competitive through a reverse bidding system.

Problem Statement

Farmers often sell their produce to local middlemen who exploit information asymmetry. For example, if the actual market price of onions is ₹35/kg, a middleman may claim that prices have fallen and offer only ₹10/kg. Since farmers usually lack access to real-time market information and alternative buyers, they are forced to accept unfair prices.

This platform aims to solve this by allowing suppliers to post procurement requirements directly, enabling multiple farmers to bid competitively while providing transparency in pricing, quality, logistics, and payments.

Reverse Procurement Workflow
Supplier posts procurement requirement.
Relevant farmers receive notifications.
Farmers submit competitive bids.
Supplier reviews all bids.
Supplier chooses Manual Selection or Smart Auto Allocation.
Orders are confirmed.
Shipment is tracked in real time.
Digital payment is completed.
Partial Fulfillment

If enabled, multiple farmers can fulfill one procurement request.

Example:

Supplier requires:

Onion
1000 kg

Farmers bid:

Farmer A → 600 kg

Farmer B → 300 kg

Farmer C → 100 kg

The supplier can accept all three bids to complete the requirement.
